 MITRA AI - Health Schemes Only
 Filtered from the previous 115-scheme corpus using a strict health-related requirement
Filtering decision
The earlier corpus included agriculture, financial aid, housing, education, livelihoods, women empowerment
and other non-health programmes. Those non-health schemes have been removed. Only schemes from the
previous corpus with a direct human-health, healthcare, medical, sickness, maternity-health or
access-to-medicines purpose are retained.
Retained schemes: 4
Removed schemes: 111
This is a strict filter of the previous list, not a claim that these are the only health schemes available in India.
For production use, current official scheme guidelines and source pages should be checked before
determining eligibility.
1. Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY)
Category: Health Care
Key benefits: Health assurance/insurance support for eligible families for covered secondary and tertiary
hospitalisation, with cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals.
Eligibility criteria: Eligibility is based on the applicable PM-JAY beneficiary identification rules and any current
expansion rules. Coverage depends on the beneficiary list and scheme conditions.
Gender eligible: All genders
Official source: Ayushman Bharat / Ayushman Arogya Mandir, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare
Source URL: https://aam.mohfw.gov.in/
2. Employees' State Insurance (ESI) Scheme
Category: Health Care
Key benefits: Medical care for insured persons and eligible family members, plus sickness, maternity,
disablement, dependants and other social-security benefits under the ESI framework.
Eligibility criteria: Employees in establishments covered by the ESI Act and within the applicable
coverage/wage rules; family coverage and individual benefits follow the scheme's conditions.
Gender eligible: All genders
Official source: Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC)
Source URL: https://www.esic.gov.in/
3. Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP)
Category: Health Care
Key benefits: Makes quality generic medicines, consumables and surgical items available at affordable
prices through Janaushadhi Kendras, helping reduce out-of-pocket medicine costs.
Eligibility criteria: For medicine access, the scheme is intended for the general public. Opening a Janaushadhi
Kendra has separate eligibility and application conditions.
Gender eligible: All genders
Official source: Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana, Department of Pharmaceuticals
Source URL: https://janaushadhi.gov.in/about-pmbjb

4. Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)
Category: Maternal & Child Health
Key benefits: Maternity cash support intended to partly compensate wage loss and promote health-seeking
behaviour and maternal/child health and nutrition among eligible pregnant women and lactating mothers.
Eligibility criteria: Pregnant women and lactating mothers belonging to the current notified socially and
economically disadvantaged categories and meeting PMMVY conditions. Benefits also cover a second child
when the second child is a girl, subject to the scheme rules.
Gender eligible: Women
Official source: Ministry of Women & Child Development - PMMVY
Source URL: https://wcd.gov.in/offerings/pradhan-mantri-matru-vandana-yojana

Why these four were retained
AB PM-JAY directly provides financial protection for covered hospital care. The ESI Scheme directly provides
medical and sickness-related benefits. PMBJP directly provides access to medicines at affordable prices.
PMMVY is a maternity-benefit programme whose stated objectives include improving health and nutrition for
mothers and children. These health links are supported by the relevant official government sources.
